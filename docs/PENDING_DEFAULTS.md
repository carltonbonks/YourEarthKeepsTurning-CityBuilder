# Claude's defaults pending Yusuf's playtest

Where CLAUDE.md left a design question open, these are the numbers and rules Claude picked so a slice could ship, with the bot results that came with them. They stand until Yusuf playtests and says otherwise; then the decision moves into CLAUDE.md (if it is design intent) and its section here is deleted. The live numbers are in `data/*.json`; when you change one that is quoted here, fix the quote too.

Add a section here (not in CLAUDE.md) when a slice ships with new defaults: area, PR and date as the heading, then the defaults and bot win rates. Sections run oldest first. Every section below is live on the playtest link and still waits for Yusuf's playtest.

## Bronze age, promotions and hero mood (slice 5, PR #9, 2026-10-11)

Bronze age, promotions and hero mood: slice 5 uses Claude's defaults (Bronze moot needs a market and 9 people and costs 14 wood, 5 stone, 40 shells (lowered 2026-10-13: the ring town left bot towns too small and short of stone to reach 12 people); copper seams for townsfolk; smelter turns 2 cassiterite into tin and 2 copper + 1 tin into a bronze ingot; ingots go to the bronzesmith's gear or to one-off tool sets per townsfolk group; the caravan sells tin at 9 and cassiterite at 5 shells, and since 2026-10-13 timber at 3 (20 a round) and stone at 4 (8 a round), so spare shells can buy building goods; bronze reavers from round 6 shrug off 40% of stone blows; a warrior with 6 kills can be promoted to a level 2 champion for 20 shells, a craftsperson with 8 goods at one workshop to a named master with a craft trait for 15; Promote cards take a dawn slot; mood is kept simple: sleeping rough once lodgings exist, hunger, cold and comrades dying lower it, a favourite bed, buying and prizes raise it, and a hero at 15 or below leaves). Pending Yusuf's playtest.

## Ring town, desire paths and gates (PRs #13 and #16, 2026-10-11/12)

Ring town, pathfinding and roads: slice 6 uses Claude's defaults (3 rings of plots in the stone age and a 4th from the bronze age, 8 sectors; 3 boulders and 2 old oaks on random plots; thickets past the town at 60% speed; trails form where feet pass most and give +20%; the stone-layers' yard costs 8 wood and 4 stone, and its craftspeople pave when nothing needs building or crafting, 1 stone per 5 squares for stone paths at +45%, 1 stone and 1 wood for bronze-age cobbles at +70%, always leaving 4 of each in stock; raiders get the road bonus too). Desire paths and gates (slice 7b) use Claude's defaults: a square walked for 3 seconds becomes a trail (+20%), a trail walked for 10 more a worn path (+35%), one stage per dawn, each fading back when walked under a quarter of that; stone-layers pave only worn paths, busiest first; a gate costs 6 wood, has 60 health per square, at most 4, mended each dawn; raiders go round when that's shorter than half a square per point of health; watch posts prefer plots near gates. Bot (150 seeds): paving from round 3 cost about 9 points, so the bot builds the yard from round 5; free gates added about 11 points, but at 6 wood the bot (gates last, after essentials) rarely has wood spare and builds about 0.1 a run, so raiders never needed to batter one. Pending Yusuf's playtest.

## Power level and catch-up (PR #15, 2026-10-12)

Power level and catch-up (2026-10-12, Yusuf's idea after Path of Exile 2's monster power): `sim/power.gd` rates every fighter as sqrt(effective HP x damage per second), so the town (heroes, warriors, hearth and towers) and each wave sit on one scale. **Yusuf decided (2026-10-12): soft catch-up that only eases waves, never boosts them, because the game shouldn't punish optimising.** Claude's defaults: when the scheduled wave is over 1.3x the town's power (1.6x until the medieval age made runs 14 rounds; see that line) as the round starts, raiders are held back from it until it isn't, keeping at least 60% of them (`catch_up_*` in data/balance.json). Bots after desire paths (60 seeds): balanced 72% to 78%, workers 23% to 40% (1.5x pushed balanced to 82%). Pending Yusuf's playtest.

## Food and watch posts (PR #18, 2026-10-13)

Food and watch posts (2026-10-12 playtest feedback): Claude's defaults: heroes eat 1 food a day like townsfolk (they always did; the UI now says so); on a dawn when someone left hungry, nobody of that role is offered at that dawn's pick (`hunger_turns_folk_away` in data/balance.json; blocking every townsfolk card cost the bot 26 points, so only the leaver's kind stays away); a garden patch (10 wood since 2026-10-14, when Yusuf found 6-wood gardens a no-brainer; max 3) brings 2 food every dawn with no workers; watch posts keep 140 apart unless they stand by a gate. Bot (60 seeds): balanced 78% to 77%, workers 40% to 62% (gardens end hunger for a town of gatherers). Pending Yusuf's playtest.

## Medieval age (PR #20, 2026-10-13)

Medieval age (2026-10-13, Yusuf approved the plan and Claude's four picks): a run is 14 rounds. The Stone keep needs the bronzesmith and 12 people and costs 20 wood, 12 stone, 4 bronze, 90 shells; it shoots (6 damage, range 170) and adds 60 to the hearth's health, and opens a 5th ring, flagstone streets (+90%), bog iron (townsfolk only), 6 watch posts instead of 4, and the medieval buildings. Charcoal burner: 3 wood into 2 charcoal (keeps 12). Bloomery: 2 bog iron + 2 charcoal into iron. Blacksmith: 1 iron + 2 charcoal into steel, and iron spears, steel longswords, mail hauberks and steel plate for the stalls. Iron tool sets cost 3 iron (ploughs: +50% gathering, +1 carry, fields twice the food; saws: +50% craft speed); mail and halberds cost 2 iron and 2 steel (+5 attack, +20 HP, cuts mail). Townhouse: room for 6, 1.5 fuel. Field (max 6): 4 food a dawn while 2 tribespeople per field are free. Family cards (2 tribespeople and a craftsperson, needs room for all three) appear from the medieval age. Guildhall: 4 beds, 1 shell dues a night; knights (proud: rough sleep hurts twice as much, steel lifts their mood by 3) cost 45 shells and need it; a level 5 champion can be knighted for 25. Chapel: 4 shells per undead, reliquaries (+4 damage per tier against undead). Threats come by round, never by age: men-at-arms with mail (halves non-steel blows) from round 10, battering rams (ignore people, batter gates and the hearth) from 11, hollow knights from the barrows from round 10 and in waves from 12, the antlered king leads round 9 and the Black Margrave round 14. Catch-up eases waves over 1.3x the town's power (was 1.6x). Bots (60 seeds, 14 rounds): balanced 68%, workers 45%, bronze 63%, medieval 68% (it reaches bronze in 41 runs and the keep in 12, from round 10, and wins 9 of those 12). Before easing catch-up to 1.3x: balanced 52%, workers 22%, bronze 55%, medieval 67%. Bot towns run short of wood, stone and food while shells pile up, so the bots reach the keep late; a human should get there sooner. Pending Yusuf's playtest.

## Building cards (2026-10-14)

Yusuf chose building cards over a grouped build menu (2026-10-14); the numbers are Claude's defaults. Each round deals 5 building cards (6 in the bronze age, 7 in the medieval), drawn without repeats. Every card has weight 1, x4 when the town needs that kind and x2 for a building the town has never had. The town needs a kind when beds are almost full (2 or fewer free), food would last under 3 days, heroes have no free bed, a stall has no workshop, a workshop has no stall, or the building is new in the current age. Ordering a card pays the usual cost and uses the card up. Basic buildings (hut, garden patch, townhouse, field: `basic` in data/buildings.json) are always on offer, as many as you can pay for. Ageing up (Bronze moot, Stone keep) is its own card whenever it can be raised. You get 1 redraw a round for 10 shells, and it leans away from the cards just shown. The bot redraws when it has 40 shells or more. Bots (60 seeds) against main: balanced 68 → 65%, workers 45 → 33%, heroes 67 → 62%, market 58 → 50%, bronze 63 → 53%, medieval 68 → 60%. Before huts and food became basics, bronze and medieval were both near 45%, because one hut or field a round starved the towns. The bot only plays the cards it is dealt and redraws by shells, never by need, so a human should lose less than this. Pending Yusuf's playtest.

## Playtest batch: towers, earlier ages, building hand (2026-10-14)

From Yusuf's 2026-10-14 playtest (seed 51705, won in round 14 with catch-up easing every attack from round 5). **Yusuf decided: sentient raiders hit back at towers; keep 14 rounds and bring the ages sooner.** Claude's defaults: a sentient raider (not a battering ram) shot by a watch post or the Stone keep goes for that tower for 8 seconds (`tower_grudge_seconds`); watch posts have 60 health and the keep 200 (`tower_hp`); a broken tower stops shooting until every tower is mended to full at dawn; a blow from a person takes the grudge back. The Bronze moot now costs 8 wood, 4 stone, 50 shells (was 14, 5, 40), adds 30 to the hearth and brings 2 bronze ingots; the Stone keep costs 10 wood, 6 stone, 4 bronze, 130 shells (was 20, 12, 4, 90) and brings 3 iron, because bot and player towns run short of wood and stone but pile up shells. Garden patches cost 10 wood (was 6). The build panel shows both power levels and says when the warband holds back. Bots (60 seeds) against main: balanced 65 → 65%, workers 33 → 28%, heroes 62 → 53%, market 50 → 68%, bronze 53 → 60% (reaches bronze in 44 runs, was 34, around round 6), medieval 60 → 52% (raises the keep in 5 runs, round 13). Holding back at most 25% of an attack instead of 40% (`catch_up_min_share` 0.75) dropped balanced to 57% and workers to 5%, so it stays at 0.6 until the wood economy is looked at: bot towns sit near 0 wood from the bronze age on, which is what keeps them from the keep. Watch posts get broken about 0.2 times a run (4 a run for the workers bot, which leans on them). Pending Yusuf's playtest.


## Gatherers plan their trips (2026-10-15)

From Yusuf's 2026-10-15 note that tribespeople set off on trips they can't finish before nightfall. Gatherers used to walk to the nearest node of the single scarcest good, all of them together, with no thought for daylight or for who else was going. Claude's defaults: each gatherer picks the trip worth the most to the town: how short the town is of the good (two days of need, minus what is in store and what others are already bringing) times what it can carry back from what the node will still hold after the others heading there take theirs, over the round trip's time. It doesn't start a trip it can't walk and gather before dusk (walking estimate padded by `gather_trip_slack` 1.15); a good the town has enough of still counts a little (`gather_need_floor` 0.05). With nothing worth fetching, a gatherer waits a second before looking again. Bots (60 seeds) against main: gathering trips given up empty-handed fell from about 55 a round (18 at dusk, 37 at an emptied node) to about 1, and goods brought in rose from about 54 to 81 a round (stone from 1.4 to 9.7). Win rates: balanced 65 → 82%, workers 28 → 60%, heroes 53 → 85%, market 68 → 77%, bronze 60 → 90%, medieval 52 → 83% (raises the keep in 39 runs, was 5). Big towns step faster too (850 people: 49 → 35 ms a step). The game is now a lot easier for the bots; whether to win that back with harder waves or leaner nodes is Yusuf's call. Pending Yusuf's playtest.
