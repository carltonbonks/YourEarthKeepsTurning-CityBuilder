# Claude's defaults pending Yusuf's playtest

Where CLAUDE.md left a design question open, these are the numbers and rules Claude picked so a slice could ship, with the bot results that came with them. They stand until Yusuf playtests and says otherwise; then the decision moves into CLAUDE.md (if it is design intent) and its line here is deleted. The live numbers are in `data/*.json`; when you change one that is quoted here, fix the quote too.

Add a line here (not in CLAUDE.md) when a slice ships with new defaults: area, date, the defaults, bot win rates.

- Hero energy and lodging rules: slice 3 uses Claude's defaults (energy drains awake and per attack, lodgings differ in rest speed, healing and how deeply heroes sleep, each trait has a favourite lodging, daily whims). Pending Yusuf's playtest.
- Bronze age, promotions and hero mood: slice 5 uses Claude's defaults (Bronze moot needs a market and 12 people and costs 14 wood, 8 stone, 30 shells; copper seams for townsfolk; smelter turns 2 cassiterite into tin and 2 copper + 1 tin into a bronze ingot; ingots go to the bronzesmith's gear or to one-off tool sets per townsfolk group; the caravan sells tin at 9 and cassiterite at 5 shells; bronze reavers from round 6 shrug off 40% of stone blows; a warrior with 6 kills can be promoted to a level 2 champion for 20 shells, a craftsperson with 8 goods at one workshop to a named master with a craft trait for 15; Promote cards take a dawn slot; mood is kept simple: sleeping rough once lodgings exist, hunger, cold and comrades dying lower it, a favourite bed, buying and prizes raise it, and a hero at 15 or below leaves). Pending Yusuf's playtest.
- Marketplace rules: slice 4 uses Claude's defaults (workshops hold 1 craftsperson and can be built twice, stalls hold 4 goods, Cheap/Fair/Dear prices per stall at x0.6/x1/x1.5, greedy heroes skip Dear stalls, crafted gear tiers up every 3 rounds, the hunting lodge pays 3 shells per beast, obsidian is fetched by heroes and sold for 2 shells a shard, between-round traders are gone). Pending Yusuf's playtest.
- Ring town, pathfinding and roads: slice 6 uses Claude's defaults (3 rings of plots in the stone age and a 4th from the bronze age, 8 sectors; 3 boulders and 2 old oaks on random plots; thickets past the town at 60% speed; trails form where feet pass most and give +20%; the stone-layers' yard costs 8 wood and 4 stone, and its craftspeople pave when nothing needs building or crafting, 1 stone per 5 squares for stone paths at +45%, 1 stone and 1 wood for bronze-age cobbles at +70%, always leaving 4 of each in stock; raiders get the road bonus too). Desire paths and gates (slice 7b) use Claude's defaults: a square walked for 3 seconds becomes a trail (+20%), a trail walked for 10 more a worn path (+35%), one stage per dawn, each fading back when walked under a quarter of that; stone-layers pave only worn paths, busiest first; a gate costs 6 wood, has 60 health per square, at most 4, mended each dawn; raiders go round when that's shorter than half a square per point of health; watch posts prefer plots near gates. Bot (150 seeds): paving from round 3 cost about 9 points, so the bot builds the yard from round 5; free gates added about 11 points, but at 6 wood the bot (gates last, after essentials) rarely has wood spare and builds about 0.1 a run, so raiders never needed to batter one. Pending Yusuf's playtest.
- Power level and catch-up (2026-10-12, Yusuf's idea after Path of Exile 2's monster power): `sim/power.gd` rates every fighter as sqrt(effective HP x damage per second), so the town (heroes, warriors, hearth and towers) and each wave sit on one scale. **Yusuf decided (2026-10-12): soft catch-up that only eases waves, never boosts them, because the game shouldn't punish optimising.** Claude's defaults: when the scheduled wave is over 1.6x the town's power as the round starts, raiders are held back from it until it isn't, keeping at least 60% of them (`catch_up_*` in data/balance.json). Bots after desire paths (60 seeds): balanced 72% to 78%, workers 23% to 40% (1.5x pushed balanced to 82%). Pending Yusuf's playtest.
- Food and watch posts (2026-10-12 playtest feedback): Claude's defaults: heroes eat 1 food a day like townsfolk (they always did; the UI now says so); on a dawn when someone left hungry, nobody of that role is offered at that dawn's pick (`hunger_turns_folk_away` in data/balance.json; blocking every townsfolk card cost the bot 26 points, so only the leaver's kind stays away); a garden patch (6 wood, max 3) brings 2 food every dawn with no workers; watch posts keep 140 apart unless they stand by a gate. Bot (60 seeds): balanced 78% to 77%, workers 40% to 62% (gardens end hunger for a town of gatherers). Pending Yusuf's playtest.
- Difficulty tuning pass 1 (2026-10-11): Claude's target is the balanced bot winning 70-80% with the workers-only bot clearly weak. One new enemy type per round (wolves round 2, cave bears 4, bone-callers 5, bronze reavers 6), softer cave bears (65 hp), tougher reavers (48 hp), hunger costs less mood (-5), more dry brush, nights 14s. Pending Yusuf's playtest.
